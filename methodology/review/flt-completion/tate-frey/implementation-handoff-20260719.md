## Kernel-clean results added

### Tate uniformization assembly

`WeierstrassCurve.tateEquivOfComponents` now assembles the public uniformization equivalence from:

1. the explicit Tate-curve point equivalence `tateCurveEquiv`; and
2. the local change of Weierstrass coordinates `exists_variableChange_tateCurve`.

The algebraic joining proof is complete and audits to the standard axiom trio. The public
`WeierstrassCurve.tateEquiv` now consumes this assembly rather than duplicating the join.

This does **not** prove its two mathematical providers. Consequently `#print axioms
WeierstrassCurve.tateEquiv` still honestly contains `sorryAx` through those admitted providers.

### Division-polynomial coprimality

`WeierstrassCurve.isCoprime_Φ_ΨSq` is proved directly over an arbitrary commutative base ring.
It no longer depends on proving the stronger universal resultant formula.

The argument is fibrewise:

1. suppose the ideal generated by `Φ n` and `ΨSq n` is proper;
2. place it inside a maximal ideal of `R[X]`;
3. pass to the quotient field;
4. the image of `X` becomes a common root of the two base-changed division polynomials;
5. contradict the existing field theorem `isCoprime_Φ_ΨSq_field`.

The exported theorem audits to exactly the standard axiom trio.
